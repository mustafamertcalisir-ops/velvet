# Raya visual analysis — actionable

Source: four reference screenshots supplied 3 Oct 2026 (a discovery card,
App Store marketing frames for Home / People / Travel, an own-profile screen,
a paused profile video). Reference only — see "Must not copy".

## What the screenshots show

| Aspect | Observation |
|---|---|
| Image-to-interface ratio | Photo/video fills ~85–90% of the screen. Interface is a thin layer at the bottom and edges. |
| Typography hierarchy | Three levels only: name + age (bold, ~22pt) → occupation (medium) → handle / city (small). Left-aligned, stacked, no labels. |
| Control density | Four round actions on a profile, two on one's own profile, five icons in the tab bar. Nothing else. |
| Spacing | Tight vertical stack of metadata (~4pt between lines), generous distance between that stack and the controls. |
| Profile information density | Name, age, occupation, location, one intent line ("Here just for friends"), optional short bio. No interest clouds, no stats. |
| Translucency | Controls and the bio panel are dark translucent surfaces over the photo; the photo stays readable through them. |
| Navigation restraint | Icon-only tab bar, monochrome; top corners hold one small control each. |
| Photography | Personal, candid, daylight, unretouched-looking — a person in their place (ivy wall, a court). Video plays in the same frame as photos. |
| Where it does NOT decorate | No borders around metadata, no badges on people, no gradients except a scrim, no brand colour in content areas, no headings like "About". |

## What we should learn

1. **The person is the screen.** Profile = full-bleed media; UI sits on its lower edge.
2. **Three typographic levels are enough** for identity: name/age, what they do, where.
3. **One intent sentence** says more than a tag cloud ("Here for friendship and community").
4. **Controls are few, quiet, and translucent** — they never compete with the face.
5. **Media is a sequence, not a slot** — photos and video share one frame and one navigation.
6. **Restraint is the luxury.** No labels, no counters, no badges on the person.
7. **Candid beats produced.** Placeholder and future brand imagery should look personal, not stock.

## What we must not copy

- The round four-button action row (undo / pass / like / send) and its icons.
- The bold-sans name block in Raya's exact stacking and sizes.
- The cyan Instagram handle under the name (our Instagram is private — DEC-023).
- The "X liked you" pill, the rounded card-inside-screen frame, the globe Travel screen.
- The translucent rounded bio panel placement, the tab-bar icon set, wording and logo.

## How this product translates it (original)

- **Name in our editorial serif** (Newsreader) over the photo — a voice Raya does not use.
- **Intent as a sentence** built from the applicant's answers, never a tag.
- **Interests as a typeset line** ("Architecture, swimming and jazz"), not chips.
- **Scroll to know more**: the lead photo fills the first frame; below it one editorial column alternates words and photographs (known for → photo → interests → photos). No tap-through carousel, no story bars, no bio panel on top of the photo, and every photograph appears once (DEC-034).
- **One media sequence** (`photo | video`), so a short video can take a frame later without new navigation.
- **No public handle**: Instagram never appears on profile surfaces.
- **Translucent surfaces only for controls** (back, the "Only you can see this" note) and the floating action.
- **Selection by typography**: chosen answers step forward in Pearl with a short rule and a drawn tick, never with an accent colour or pills (DEC-039).
