# PRODUCT DEFINITION

## Working Definition

This project is an original private-membership dating and social discovery product focused initially on Türkiye.

The product is not intended to maximize registrations.

It is intended to create a high-trust, high-signal community in which members feel that:
- people have been meaningfully reviewed
- profiles are considered rather than disposable
- privacy is respected
- the environment feels culturally rich rather than transactional
- the community is more valuable than endless volume

---

## Product Promise

A private community of people worth meeting.

This is not a wealth statement.

"People worth meeting" means people who may be memorable because of:
- their work
- craft
- perspective
- taste
- curiosity
- achievement
- creativity
- generosity
- social trust
- contribution to a community

---

## What Makes the Product Different

### 1. Membership is earned through review
Downloading the app does not provide immediate access.

### 2. Application before dating profile
The product first evaluates membership fit.

### 3. Community before volume
The goal is not unlimited swiping.

### 4. Privacy as product value
Sensitive identity and social information is handled carefully.

### 5. Editorial profile presentation
Member profiles should feel composed and intentional.

### 6. Türkiye-first social context
The product should understand local city culture, travel patterns, and social behavior without turning into a cliché of "Turkish luxury."

---

## Product Is Not

- Tinder with expensive colors
- a rich-person leaderboard
- a celebrity directory
- a follower-count contest
- a nightlife promoter app
- an influencer marketplace
- a recruitment network
- a generic social network
- a generic subscription dating app

---

## High-End Definition

High-end means:

- fewer but better-considered interactions
- meaningful admission
- polished typography
- excellent photography
- quiet UI
- strong privacy
- excellent motion
- reliable engineering
- intelligent moderation
- intentional community design
- thoughtful copy

High-end does not mean visible ostentation.

---

## Initial Geographic Focus

Türkiye first.

Priority cities may include:
- İstanbul
- İzmir
- Ankara
- Antalya
- Bodrum / Muğla region

The architecture must still support:
- international phone numbers
- international residents
- Turkish citizens living abroad
- travelers
- additional countries later

Do not hard-code the product as Türkiye-only.

## Community Principle

Do not reduce community value to occupation prestige.

A less famous person with a distinctive craft or perspective may add more to the community than a high-status person with no meaningful contribution.

---

## Membership Psychology

The product should create:

- anticipation without manipulation
- selectivity without arrogance
- privacy without secrecy theatre
- confidence without vanity
- aspiration without wealth worship

---

## Admission Philosophy

The initial application should be quick enough to complete comfortably.

The extended application should be requested only after the applicant has progressed sufficiently in review.

This creates a meaningful distinction between:
- initial intent
- serious consideration
- full evaluation

---

## Referral Philosophy

Referrals help establish familiarity.

They do not:
- guarantee admission
- replace identity checks
- create public status
- create referral leaderboards

Applicants without referrals must still be able to apply.

---

## Member Product Vision

After approval, the product may eventually include:

### Home
Curated introductions rather than endless inventory.

### People
Member discovery.

### Directory
Search by relevant high-level attributes and interests.

### Places
Social presence around selected neighborhoods, venues, and moments.

### Travel
Future city presence and travel plans.

### Messages
Private, respectful communication.

### Gatherings
Curated small-scale real-world experiences.

---

## Core Success Metrics

Do not optimize only for:
- swipe count
- time spent
- profile inventory
- push notification opens

Prefer metrics such as:
- application completion quality
- verification success
- approved-member retention
- meaningful mutual connections
- conversation quality
- safety reports per active member
- response health
- successful offline connection signals
- privacy trust
- referral quality
- member satisfaction

---

## Product Principle

Quality should increase as the community grows.

Scale must not destroy the reason people joined.
