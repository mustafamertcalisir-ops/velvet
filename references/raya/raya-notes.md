# RAYA REFERENCE NOTES

These notes describe principles to study from Raya-like references.

They are not implementation instructions to clone the product.

---

# What to Study

## 1. Photography Dominance
Profiles feel like media first and interface second.

Principle:
Let strong member photography carry emotional impact.

---

## 2. Small Metadata
Name, age, occupation, location, and handle can remain visually secondary.

Principle:
Do not turn profiles into résumés.

---

## 3. Quiet Controls
Controls often sit on translucent/dark surfaces and avoid excessive decoration.

Principle:
Controls should remain available without becoming the visual subject.

---

## 4. Full-Screen Presence
Profile imagery is immersive.

Principle:
Design around the person rather than around UI cards.

---

## 5. Restrained Navigation
Navigation is visually quiet.

Principle:
Reduce chrome and preserve focus.

---

## 6. Dark / Neutral Foundation
Neutral UI allows photography to provide most of the color.

Principle:
Use brand accents sparingly.

---

## 7. Curated Community Psychology
The product feels different primarily because access is controlled.

Principle:
Do not try to recreate exclusivity through visual luxury alone.

---

# What NOT to Copy

Do not copy:
- logo
- app name
- proprietary copy
- exact profile layout
- exact iconography
- exact nav layout
- exact button arrangement
- exact animations
- exact color values
- exact typography
- admission wording

---

# Translation Into Our Product

Our product should be:
- slightly warmer
- more Türkiye-aware
- privacy-forward
- culturally sophisticated
- less celebrity-coded
- less dependent on social-media status
- original in brand identity

---

# Visual Rule

Before implementing any reference-inspired feature:

1. Explain the design principle.
2. Explain why it is useful.
3. Rebuild the principle using our own tokens/components/layout.
4. Compare the result for originality.
